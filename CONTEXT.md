# Gift exchange

The language of the gift exchange **Tool** on `swavely.lol`, which replaces Drawnames for
the family. People, sign-in and **Managed** People belong to the Hub
(`../swavely-lol/CONTEXT.md`). This file is a glossary, not a spec.

## Language

**Group**:
A circle of People who exchange gifts every year: one side of the family, a set of
friends. Recurs; nothing about it is tied to a year.
_Avoid_: Family, party, event

**Organizer**:
A Person who runs one **Group**: adds and removes its People, sets its rules, starts
each **Exchange** and runs the draw. A Group may have several. The Hub Admin creates a
Group and names its first Organizer.
_Avoid_: Admin, owner, host

**Exchange**:
One year of a **Group**: "Swavely Christmas 2026". The draw happens here, and the
"not the same as last year" rule compares against the same Group's previous Exchange,
never another Group's. Has a **gift date** and, optionally, a **budget** ("about $50").
Is **open** (Participants chosen, lists updated), then **drawn**, then **done** once the
gift date passes, when its Pairings become next year's "last year".
_Avoid_: Draw (that is what happens in an Exchange), event, round

**Participant**:
A Person taking part in one **Exchange**. Being in a Group does not make someone a
Participant every year; sitting a year out leaves their history intact.
_Avoid_: Member, player, user

### Wish lists

**Wish list**:
What one Person would like, kept from year to year and shown to everyone who shares a
**Group** with them, not only their **Giver**. One per Person, whatever Groups they are
in. Kept by its Person, or by whoever manages them.
_Avoid_: Gift list, registry, wishlist (one word)

**Item**:
One thing on a **Wish list**: a name, and optionally a link, a note and a rough price.
_Avoid_: Gift, wish, product

**Claim**:
"I'm getting this," set on an **Item** by anyone who can see it, so two people do not
buy the same thing. Hidden from the Item's own Person; shown to everyone else who sees
the list, including whoever manages that Person.
_Avoid_: Reservation, purchased, bought, taken

### The draw

**Pairing**:
One **Giver** and the one **Recipient** they buy for, in one **Exchange**. Every
Participant is in exactly two: once giving, once receiving.
_Avoid_: Match, assignment, match-up

**Giver** / **Recipient**:
The two ends of a **Pairing**. "Who did you get?" asks for your Recipient.
_Avoid_: Santa, drawer, drawee, giftee

**Draw**:
The act of making every **Pairing** in an **Exchange** at once, at random, within its
rules. Run by an **Organizer**. After it the Pairings change only by **Re-pairing** or a
**Redraw**.
_Avoid_: Shuffle, match, randomise

**Redraw**:
A whole new **Draw** for an Exchange already drawn, every Pairing made again and every
Giver told to look again. The answer when someone draws a person they had last year and
the year before that is not on record.
_Avoid_: Reroll, reshuffle

**Exclusion**:
One way, within one **Group**: "Nathan never draws his wife." Says nothing about the
other direction, which is its own Exclusion; spouses are two. Set by an Organizer.
Belongs to the Group, so a friends' Group never inherits a family's.
_Avoid_: Household (there are none), block, restriction, constraint

**Repeat**:
A **Giver** drawn the same **Recipient** as in the Group's previous Exchange (last year
only; two years ago is fine). The **Draw** avoids Repeats, and allows the fewest it can
only when an Organizer agrees that no draw exists without them. Self-draws, Exclusions
and Fixed pairings are never bent.
_Avoid_: Duplicate, same-as-last-year

**Fixed pairing**:
A **Pairing** an Organizer sets before the **Draw** ("Mom always has Grandpa"), which the
Draw keeps and works around.
_Avoid_: Override, lock, manual match

**Past pairing**:
A **Pairing** from an Exchange that happened somewhere else, entered by hand so the
rules know who gave to whom (Drawnames years, chiefly). History, not a draw.
_Avoid_: Import, legacy draw

**Sealed**:
The state of an Exchange's Pairings once drawn: each Giver, and whoever manages them,
sees their own Recipient and nothing else. Organizers and the Hub Admin included: they
are usually Participants, and they keep their surprise too. A wrong Pairing is mended
by **Re-pairing**, which needs nobody to look.
_Avoid_: Hidden, private, secret

**Re-pairing**:
Changing a sealed **Exchange** after the **Draw** because someone leaves or joins. The
Organizer names who changed; the Tool re-pairs only those it must and tells each
affected Giver their new Recipient. Nobody sees the others.
_Avoid_: Redraw (that is every Pairing again), swap, override
