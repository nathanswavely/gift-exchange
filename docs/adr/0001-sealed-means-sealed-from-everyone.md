# Sealed means sealed from everyone

## Status

accepted (2026-10-18)

## Decision

Once an **Exchange** is drawn, a **Giver** (and whoever manages them) sees their own
**Recipient** and nothing else. No screen shows anyone else's Pairing: not to an
**Organizer**, not to the Hub Admin.

- Every fix after the draw is made without looking. **Re-pairing** takes who left or
  joined and re-pairs only those it must; **Redraw** makes every Pairing again. Either
  way each affected Giver is emailed to sign in, and the email never carries a name.
- When the rules leave no draw without a **Repeat**, the Organizer is told so and may
  allow the fewest Repeats, but is never told who repeated.

## Context

Drawnames lets the organizer peek. In a family the organizer is a Participant, so a
peek spoils their own surprise and, worse, makes everyone wonder whether they did. The
Hub Admin is a Participant too. The one honest promise is that the screen cannot show
it.

## Consequences

- There is no "view all pairings" page to build, and none to secure.
- Someone who must look can still read the database by hand. That is a deliberate act
  outside the Tool, not a button.
